Our Story

Brew & Bean began with a simple wish: a warm corner of the city where a carefully extracted espresso could slow the day down. We roast and brew with patience, source beans we would drink ourselves, and keep the lights low enough for conversation to linger.

Every cup is a small ritual — from the first crackle of the grinder to the last sip. Whether you come to work, to meet, or simply to sit, we make space for you to feel at home.
